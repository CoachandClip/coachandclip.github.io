Privacy Policy

Coach&Stats does not require a user account or login.

Basketball information entered into the app, including team names, player names, jersey numbers, game information and statistics, is used to provide the app's statistics and game management features.

Coach&Stats does not sell user information and does not use personal information for advertising or marketing purposes.

Team, player and game information is stored using Apple's application storage technologies.

When you choose to export or share a box score or scoresheet, information is shared only through the destination you select using Apple's standard sharing features.

Users are responsible for ensuring they have appropriate authority to enter player information into the app.
